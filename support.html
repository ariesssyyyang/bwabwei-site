常見問題 Frequently asked

怎麼擲筊？ How do I toss?

心中默念問題，然後點下方的筊杯按鈕，或直接搖一搖手機。兩片筊杯落地停好後，畫面上方會顯示結果。

Think of your question, then tap the moon-block button at the bottom or simply shake your phone. Once both blocks come to rest, the result appears at the top.

聖筊、笑筊、陰筊是什麼意思？ What do the results mean?

聖筊：一平一凸，神明應允。笑筊：兩面皆平，神明笑而不答，請再問一次。陰筊：兩面皆凸，神明不允。

聖筊 (one flat, one round): yes. 笑筊 (both flat): the gods are laughing — ask again. 陰筊 (both round): no.

為什麼出現「立筊了，再擲一次」？ Why does it say to toss again?

筊杯偶爾會立起來或卡在邊上，這時看不出哪一面朝上，請再擲一次。如果筊杯一直晃動沒有停下，也會請你重擲。

Now and then a block lands standing on its edge, so neither face is up — just toss again. You’ll also be asked to toss again if the blocks keep rocking and never settle.

搖晃不太靈敏或太敏感？ Shaking doesn’t trigger, or triggers too easily?

輕快地甩一下手機就能觸發。如果還是沒反應，可以直接點下方的筊杯按鈕。

A quick flick of the phone is enough. If it still doesn’t respond, tap the moon-block button instead.

「平安」打賞是什麼？ What is 平安?

從畫面右側拉出的「平安」是自願打賞，用來支持本 App 的開發，不會解鎖任何功能。款項由 Apple 處理；如需退款，請透過 reportaproblem.apple.com 向 Apple 申請。

平安, pulled out from the right edge, is a voluntary tip that supports the app’s development; it doesn’t unlock anything. Payments are handled by Apple — for a refund, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

我的資料安全嗎？ Is my data private?

本 App 不收集任何資料，詳見隱私權政策。

The app collects no data at all. See the Privacy Policy.
