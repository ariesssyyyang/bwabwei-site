隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

簡單來說：Bwa Bwei 不收集任何你的資料。沒有帳號、沒有伺服器，開發者看不到你的任何資訊。

The short version: Bwa Bwei does not collect your data. There is no account and no server, and the developer cannot see any of your information.

關於本 App Who we are

Bwa Bwei（以下稱「本 App」）是一款在 iPhone 上擲筊的 App。本政策說明本 App 如何處理你的資訊，由本 App 的開發者（以下稱「我們」）提供。

Bwa Bwei (“the app”) is a moon-block (擲筊) app for iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles your information. It is provided by the app’s developer (“we”, “us”).

本 App 處理的資訊 Information the app handles

本 App 不需要登入，也不會要求你輸入任何個人資料。擲筊的結果只顯示在畫面上，不會被儲存或傳送。

The app has no login and never asks for personal information. Toss results are only shown on screen; they are not stored or sent anywhere.

動作感測 Motion sensors

你可以搖晃手機來擲筊。本 App 會讀取手機的動作感測器來偵測搖晃，這些資料只在裝置上即時計算，不會被儲存，也不會離開你的手機。

You can shake your phone to toss. The app reads the motion sensors to detect the shake. This data is processed on the device in real time; it is never stored and never leaves your phone.

App 內購買 In-app purchases

「平安」打賞是透過 Apple 的 App 內購買完成。付款與帳單資訊完全由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資料，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。打賞是自願的，不會解鎖任何功能。

Tips (平安) are made through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Payment and billing are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. Apple’s Privacy Policy applies. Tips are voluntary and do not unlock any features.

沒有追蹤、分析或廣告 No tracking, analytics or ads

本 App 不含任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤工具。我們不會建立你的個人檔案，也不會與第三方分享任何資料。

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not build profiles of you, and we do not share any data with third parties.

兒童 Children

本 App 並非以兒童為對象，也不會蓄意收集任何人的個人資料。

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

政策變更 Changes to this policy

我們可能會不定期更新本政策，頁首的「最後更新」日期即為目前版本。

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the current version.
